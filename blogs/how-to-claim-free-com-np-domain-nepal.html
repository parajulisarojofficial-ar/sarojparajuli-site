How Nepali Citizens Can Claim a Free .com.np Domain

Posted on: November 7, 2026
• 4 min read •
Author: Saroj Parajuli

Every Nepali citizen can claim a lifetime free .com.np domain through Mercantile Communication's official portal (register.com.np).

Owning your name domain (like sarojparajuli.com.np) builds your personal brand, improves organic search visibility, establishes authentic credibility with clients and recruiters, and gives you a permanent, sovereign home for your portfolio, blogs, and custom email at zero registration or renewal cost.

Why You Should Claim Your Personal .com.np Domain

  • ✓ 100% Free Forever: Zero registration fee and zero annual renewal fees.
  • ✓ Identity Protection: Prevents others from registering your official legal name.
  • ✓ Professional Resume: Stand out to universities, employers, and international recruiters.
  • ✓ Local SEO Advantage: Prioritized by Google for search queries originating in Nepal.

Step-by-Step Registration Guide

01

Check Name Availability

Your domain must strictly match your legal name as printed on your Nepali citizenship certificate (e.g. firstnamelastname.com.np or initials). Search availability on the official WHOIS lookup page.

02

Create & Verify Your Account

Sign up with your personal email on the registration portal. Click the verification link sent to your inbox to activate your account.

03

Configure DNS Nameservers

Before submitting, specify your primary and secondary nameservers (for instance, Cloudflare's ns1.cloudflare.com, or your Vercel/hosting DNS nameservers).

04

Upload Verification Documents

Upload clear scans of your Nepali Citizenship Certificate (front and back) and a signed cover letter (JPG/PNG under 800 KB). Approval typically takes 1 to 2 business days.

Sample Cover Letter

You can copy this standard template, fill in your details, print it, sign it with pen, and upload a clear photo or scan:
