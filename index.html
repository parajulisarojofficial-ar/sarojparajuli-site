Journey in Statistics & Numbers

I am currently in my final year of Bachelor of Science in Statistics at Tri-Chandra Multiple Campus, affiliated with Tribhuvan University (TU), in Kathmandu, Nepal. My academic journey revolves around probability theory, inferential modeling, sampling methodologies, and data analysis.

Rather than viewing statistics purely through dry formulas, I view data as a lens for genuine storytelling and decision-making. Whether building structured questionnaires with KoboToolbox & XLSForm or cleaning datasets in Python and PostgreSQL, I focus on accuracy, methodology, and practical applicability.

Acoustic Music & Creative Life

Beyond datasets and research forms, my creative sanctuary is the acoustic guitar. Late-night chord progressions, Nepali folk melodies, and fingerstyle recordings keep me grounded and curious. This website serves as a living home for both my technical aspirations and my musical explorations.

Professional Overview

I am an analytical thinker and aspiring Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E) / Data Analyst with a rigorous foundation in quantitative methodology. My core expertise sits at the intersection of survey design, data quality verification, and statistical analysis.

During my academic journey at Tri-Chandra Multiple Campus (TU), I have developed hands-on capabilities in designing digitized questionnaires using KoboToolbox and XLSForm syntax (including complex skip logic, regex validations, calculate fields, and repeat groups). I believe that clean data at the collection stage is the bedrock of trustworthy statistical inference.

Technically, I work comfortably with Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib) for data munging and exploratory analysis, SQL and PostgreSQL for relational database querying, and advanced Excel for quick reporting and pivot analyses.

Data Integrity
